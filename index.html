Dream Band
Music Rhythm Game

Dream Band is a freemium music rhythm game by Tap Studio. It is designed as a simple and accessible experience that players can enjoy as is.

About the Game

Dream Band brings players into a rhythm-based gameplay experience centered on music, timing, and performance. The game is built to offer easy access to basic features at no cost while delivering engaging play for rhythm game fans.

What Players Can Expect

  • Music-based rhythm gameplay
  • A freemium game experience
  • Accessible play with core features available at no cost
  • Support from platform and service integrations used by the app

App Experience

To support gameplay and app functionality, Dream Band may work with device-related information such as location and device ID on the user's device. The app may also rely on third-party services and libraries to help provide a smoother service experience.

Supported Services

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob
  • Godot
